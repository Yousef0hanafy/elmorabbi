# Reference Analysis & Market Landscape

*Note: Based on inferred analysis of Islamic habit trackers vs LMS systems.*

## Current Landscape
Most Islamic apps (e.g., Muslim Pro, Athan) focus on utility (prayer times, Qibla) or solitary habit tracking. Educational platforms (e.g., Moodle) are too academic and not suited for daily micro-habits. 

## What's Missing in the Market
- **The "Tarbiyah" (Mentorship) Element:** An interface connecting a spiritual guide with the student's daily data.
- **Holistic View:** Combining knowledge (memorization/assignments) with practice (daily deeds).

## Key Learnings to Apply
- **Information Architecture:** Must separate "Daily Action" (what I need to do today) from "Long-term Progress" (how I am doing).
- **Interaction Patterns:** Logging a daily deed must be frictionless (one-tap). If it takes more than 2 seconds to log a prayer, students will abandon the app.
- **Visual Language:** Calm, trustworthy, and distraction-free. Avoid aggressive red notifications; use supportive, encouraging design language.
