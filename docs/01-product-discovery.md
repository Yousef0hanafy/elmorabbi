# Product Discovery

## Core Concept
A structured, teacher-guided Islamic habit-building and educational platform. Unlike standalone habit trackers, this platform focuses on accountability, mentorship, and personalized growth under the supervision of a Sheikh/Murabbi.

## Value Proposition
- **For Students:** Structured spiritual growth, consistent accountability, and personalized feedback.
- **For Teachers (Sheikhs):** Efficient oversight of multiple students, actionable insights into student progress, and centralized communication.

## Personas
1. **The Seeker (Student/Talib):** Wants to improve daily religious habits (prayer, quran, adhkar) but struggles with consistency. Values feedback and feels motivated by a teacher's oversight.
2. **The Mentor (Sheikh/Murabbi):** Guides dozens of students. Needs to quickly identify who is falling behind and who is excelling. Values dashboards and exception-based reporting over raw data dumps.
3. **The Administrator:** Manages the platform, onboards Sheikhs, and oversees global metrics.

## Challenged Assumptions
- *Assumption:* Gamification (streaks, badges, points) is necessary. 
  *Challenge:* Over-gamification can detract from the sincerity (Ikhlas) of religious deeds. We should prioritize *progress visualization* and *teacher encouragement* over superficial badges.
- *Assumption:* The Sheikh needs to see every single action. 
  *Challenge:* The Sheikh will be overwhelmed. The system should aggregate data and highlight anomalies (e.g., "Student X missed Fajr for 3 consecutive days").
