# Open Decisions & Questions

Before we write any production code, I need your decisions on the following critical questions:

1. **Onboarding Flow:** Do students sign up freely and request a Sheikh, or does a Sheikh create an invite link for their specific students? *(I recommend invite links for MVP to prevent spam).*
2. **Task Flexibility:** Should the MVP strictly use fixed tasks (5 Prayers, Adhkar, etc.) or should Sheikhs be able to create custom tasks from Day 1? *(I recommend fixed for MVP speed).*
3. **Gender/Privacy Requirements:** Are there strict privacy requirements regarding communication between male/female students and teachers that need to be enforced at the app level?
4. **Gamification Strategy:** Do you agree with the recommendation to avoid heavy gamification (points/badges) in favor of simple streak tracking and direct teacher encouragement?
5. **Hosting & Tech Stack:** Do you approve of the Next.js + Supabase + Vercel stack?
